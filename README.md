# Last Merchant

<p align="center">
  <img src="Assets/Screenshots/splashArt.png" alt="Last Merchant Splash Art" width="500">
</p>

You are the last human who can stop the final collapse of galactic civilization. Forgotten planets drift in the dark, inhabited by bizarre creatures. Your task is to keep them alive through strategic barter trading. Every second of delay brings the galaxy closer to extinction.

In this demanding game, you must demonstrate excellent planning and spatial orientation. Finding your way back in the randomly generated void depends solely on your navigation skills.

Survive until the end of time to save what remains of our civilization. Good luck, Last Merchant...

### About the Project
This game was developed by **CURSED CODE** during a 24-hour game jam as part of the student game development project **Cyberiada 2025/2026**.
The theme of the game jam was "The last human nearby".

### Key Features
* Strategic barter trading system
* Randomly generated space void exploration
* Demanding navigation and planning challenges
* Unique retro terminal-style graphics
